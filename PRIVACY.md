# Leave Calendar: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 8 November 2026. Draft: replace the contact details before publishing._

Leave Calendar helps you track your leave. We built it so that we never receive your data.

## What the app stores, and where

- **On your device:** the days you mark (leave type, title, notes), holidays you add or change, your leave
  settings and balances you type in, plus app preferences such as light/dark appearance. This is kept in the
  app's private storage on your phone or tablet.
- **In your Google Drive (only if you sign in with Google):** a copy of the same calendar data, kept in a private,
  hidden app folder in **your own** Google Drive. Leave Calendar can't see any of your other Drive files, and
  other apps can't see this folder.

Leave Calendar has **no servers**. We don't collect, receive, sell or share your data. The app has no analytics,
advertising or tracking.

## Google sign-in

Signing in is optional. If you sign in, the app asks Google for:

- your **name and email address**, shown in Settings so you know which account you're backing up to, and
- permission to **see, create and delete its own data in your Google Drive** (the `drive.appdata` scope),
  used only to back up and sync your calendar.

Data moves directly between your device and Google over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Google's handling of your
account is covered by Google's own privacy policy. Leave Calendar's use of information received from Google APIs
follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Deleting your data

- **Settings → Saving → Delete backup** removes the calendar from your Google Drive and from the device, and
  removes the app's access to your Google account.
- **Sign out** removes the calendar from the device; it stays in your Google Drive.
- You can also delete the app's Drive data yourself: Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Leave Calendar →
  *Delete hidden app data*. Remove the app's access at
  [myaccount.google.com/connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).
- Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on the device.

## Children

Leave Calendar is a work tool and isn't directed at children.
